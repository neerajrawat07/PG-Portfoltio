Our Story

Honoring the art of coffee

For nine years, the Brew Elite Awards have recognized the world's most exceptional coffee brands — from single-origin roasters in Ethiopia to innovative sustainability leaders in Scandinavia.

Every bean tells a story. Our rigorous judging process evaluates flavor profile, sourcing ethics, roasting precision, and brand impact across 12 countries and 200+ submissions annually.
